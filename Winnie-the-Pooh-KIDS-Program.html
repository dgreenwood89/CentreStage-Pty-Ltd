Welcome

Welcome to the Hundred Acre Wood.

Thank you for joining us for our Junior Troupe's production of Disney's Winnie the Pooh KIDS. For many of tonight's performers, this is their very first time on stage — and every one of them has worked hard all term to bring Pooh and his friends to life.

Theatre at this age is about confidence, friendship and the joy of telling a story together. We hope you leave with a smile, and perhaps a craving for honey.

Please sit back, cheer loudly, and enjoy the show.

David Greenwood OAM
Managing Director, CentreStage
The story

A rescue mission, with snacks.

Welcome to the Hundred Acre Wood, where Winnie the Pooh is once again in search of honey. Along the way, he meets his pals Tigger, Piglet, Rabbit and Owl — but soon discovers that Christopher Robin has been captured by the mysterious Backson.

As they prepare for a rescue operation, the animals learn about teamwork, friendship and, of course, sharing snacks.
