Colouring competition terms and conditions
  1. The competition is run by CentreStage Pty Ltd, Unit 2, 255 Hamilton Highway, Fyansford VIC 3218.
  2. Open to children aged 1 to 16 at the time of entry. Entries must be submitted with the permission of a parent or guardian.
  3. One entry per child. Entries must be the child's own work, on the official colouring sheet.
  4. Enter by uploading at centrestage.org.au/comp, by post or drop-off to the address above, or through a participating school. Entries close 5pm Wednesday 16 December 2026. Late, incomplete or illegible entries are not eligible.
  5. Four winners will be drawn at random by the cast of FROZEN at 7pm on Wednesday 16 December 2026 and notified by email by Sunday 20 December 2026. Winners may be announced by first name and suburb on Facebook @centrestageaus.
  6. Each prize is one family pass of four tickets (two adults and two youth) to opening night, Friday 5 March 2027, 7:30pm, The Playhouse, Geelong Arts Centre. Youth tickets are for children aged 16 and under, who must be accompanied by an adult. Seats are allocated by CentreStage.
  7. Prizes are not transferable, exchangeable or redeemable for cash. If a winner cannot be contacted within 14 days, or cannot attend, a redraw may take place.
  8. Entries are not returned. With permission given on entry, artwork and the child's first name may be shared on CentreStage social media. Personal details are used only to run this competition and are not shared.
