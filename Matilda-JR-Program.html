Welcome

Sometimes you have to be a little bit naughty.

Thank you for joining us for our Middle Troupes' production of Roald Dahl's Matilda The Musical JR. Across three classes and almost one hundred young performers, our Middles have spent the term learning that a small person with a big idea can change everything.

Every student on stage tonight has worked on their singing, acting and movement, and just as importantly on being a generous, reliable member of a company. That is the real lesson of theatre, and they have learned it well.

Please sit back, cheer loudly, and enjoy the revolution.

David Greenwood OAM
Managing Director, CentreStage
The story

A little girl with a lot of nerve.

Matilda Wormwood is an extraordinary girl with a vivid imagination, a love of books and a sharp mind. Her parents ignore her, and her new school is run by the terrifying headmistress, Miss Trunchbull.

With the help of her kind teacher, Miss Honey, and some brave new friends, Matilda discovers a remarkable power of her own, and finds the courage to stand up to the grown-ups who have it wrong.

Three classes, one show
Middles A, B and C each perform their own production.

Each class has its own cast, listed on the pages that follow.

About the show

Based on the much-loved novel by Roald Dahl, Matilda The Musical was first produced by the Royal Shakespeare Company and has since won more than 100 international awards. Matilda JR. is a shortened version created especially for young performers.

About the writers

Book by Dennis Kelly, with music and lyrics by Australian songwriter and comedian Tim Minchin, who grew up in Perth. Orchestrations and additional music by Chris Nightingale.
