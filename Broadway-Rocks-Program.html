Welcome

Tonight, they rock.

Welcome to Broadway Rocks, the end of year showcase for the CentreStage Performing Arts Academy. Tonight, every class in our Saturday program takes the Costa Hall stage together, singing the rock anthems of musical theatre.

Our students have spent the year building their skills in acting, singing and movement. This is their moment to share it with you. There are no costumes or sets tonight, only voices, projections and a lot of courage. Thank you for being here, for cheering, and for every Saturday drop-off that made tonight possible.

David Greenwood OAMManaging Director
