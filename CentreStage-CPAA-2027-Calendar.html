Before term one

How the year works.

Invoices & fees

Term 1 invoices are issued the week beginning Sunday 3 January for continuing students, and Wednesday 27 January for new students.

Fees are due on the first Saturday of each term. A 10% late fee applies to any account unpaid after the due date.

The CentreStage offices are closed 1 – 18 January. Enquiries sent in that window are answered from Tuesday 19 January.

Enrolments for 2028 open Sunday 28 November 2027.

Attendance & withdrawals

No classes on Victorian public holidays, or on a Saturday inside a long weekend — where the holiday falls on the Friday before or Monday after.

Class fees are not refundable for students who withdraw mid-term, and unpaid fees for that term remain payable.

This is what holds our rate at an average $5.70 per hour — the lowest in Geelong.

Permissions & volunteering

Permission slips for the Frozen excursion and Pako Festa go out Saturday 6 February and are due back Saturday 20 February, by Google Form.

Families contribute 10+ volunteer hours across the year, with an opt-out available at onboarding.

Our program is designed and led by VIT registered teachers and industry professionals. All staff hold a current Working with Children Check.

What to bring

Clothes you can move in, closed-toe shoes, a full drink bottle and a snack. Hair tied back. Your CentreStage shirt on performance days.

Where things live

Class updates, absences and accounts are all in Studio Pro. Tickets for every student production are at centrestage.org.au/tickets.
