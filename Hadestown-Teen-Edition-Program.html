Welcome

Raise your cups to the ones who sing.

Thank you for joining us for our Senior Troupes' production of Hadestown: Teen Edition. Across four classes and 88 young performers, our Seniors have taken on one of the most demanding and beautiful scores in contemporary musical theatre.

Hadestown asks its company to sing as one voice, to hold a story with stillness, and to trust each other completely. Our Seniors have done exactly that, in rehearsal rooms full of focus, generosity and real artistry.

Please sit back and enjoy the song. It's an old song, and we're going to sing it again.

David Greenwood OAM
Managing Director, CentreStage
The story

Way down under the ground.

Orpheus is a poor young songwriter. Eurydice is a hungry, hard-travelled girl looking for a place to stay. When they fall in love, the world above is harsh and the winter is long.

Far below, Hades rules a mighty industrial underworld, where work is promised and freedom is not. When Eurydice is tempted down to Hadestown, Orpheus sets out to bring her home, armed only with a song.

Narrated by Hermes and watched over by the three Fates, it's a story of love, hope and doubt, and of what it takes to walk the road out of hell.

Four classes, one show
Seniors A, B, C and D each perform their own production.

About the show

Hadestown opened on Broadway in 2019 and won eight Tony Awards, including Best Musical, along with the Grammy Award for Best Musical Theater Album. Hadestown: Teen Edition is an adaptation created especially for young performers.

About the writer

Written by American singer-songwriter Anaïs Mitchell, who first created Hadestown as a folk opera, co-conceived with Ben T. Matchstick. The original New York production was developed with and directed by Rachel Chavkin.
